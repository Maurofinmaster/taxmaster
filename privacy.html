1. INTRODUCTION

Tax Master ("App," "we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play Store and other distribution platforms.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

Important: Tax Master is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute professional tax advice. You remain solely responsible for the accuracy and completeness of any information submitted through this App and for filing accurate tax returns with the Belgian Federal Public Service Finance (SPF Finances).

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Anonymous Usage - No Personal Identification

Tax Master operates completely anonymously. We do NOT collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • ❌ Your name
  • ❌ Email address
  • ❌ Phone number
  • ❌ Belgian identification number (rijksregisternummer)
  • ❌ Bank account information
  • ❌ Home address
  • ❌ Any other information that could identify you personally

You can use Tax Master completely anonymously without creating an account or providing any personal details.

2.2 Tax-Related Data (Stored Locally Only)

When you use Tax Master, you enter tax-related information for calculation purposes:

  • Income information (salary, self-employment income, rental income, etc.)
  • Professional expenses
  • Personal deductions
  • Family status and dependent information
  • Property information (for property-related deductions)

Critical Privacy Protection:

  • ✅ All tax data is stored ONLY on your device
  • ✅ We NEVER transmit this data to our servers
  • ✅ We NEVER see or access your tax information
  • ✅ Your financial data never leaves your device

This means your sensitive tax information remains private and under your complete control.

2.3 Anonymous Technical Data (Firebase)

To improve app stability and performance, we collect limited anonymized technical data via Firebase (Google). This data is used exclusively to detect crashes, improve app quality, and understand which features are most used — without ever identifying you personally.

The following anonymized data may be collected:

  • Device model and Android OS version
  • App version
  • Anonymized crash reports and error logs (no personal or tax data included)
  • Anonymized feature usage (e.g., which screens are visited, session duration)
  • Anonymized device identifier

Important: This data is collected in aggregate and cannot be linked back to you personally. Your tax data and personal information are never included in these reports.

This data is collected through:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymized crash and error reporting
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymized usage patterns to improve the app

If you wish to limit this data collection, you can do so through your Android device settings under Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy, or by uninstalling the app.

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Personal Identity: No names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses
  • ❌ Location Data: Tax Master does not request or collect your location
  • ❌ Camera/Microphone: We do not access your camera, microphone, or photos
  • ❌ Contacts: We do not access your contact list
  • ❌ Tax Data on Servers: Your entered tax information never leaves your device
  • ❌ Rijksregisternummer or Bank Accounts: We never collect Belgian ID numbers or financial account details

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Core Functionality: Tax data you enter is stored locally on your device only
  • Improve the App: Anonymized Firebase analytics data and crash reports help us identify bugs and improve features
  • Fix Bugs & Technical Issues: Anonymized crash logs and device information help us resolve stability issues

We Will NOT:

  • ❌ Sell, rent, or trade your data (we don't have personal data to sell)
  • ❌ Share your tax information with anyone (it never leaves your device)
  • ❌ Use your data for marketing purposes
  • ❌ Profile you for insurance, credit, or employment purposes
  • ❌ Create user accounts or track individual users
  • ❌ Link your tax data to any personal identifier

4. DATA RETENTION

  • Tax Data (Entered by You): Stored locally on your device until you uninstall the app. We never receive or store this data on our servers.
  • Anonymized Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics): Retained for 90 days, then automatically deleted by Firebase
  • Anonymized Analytics Data (Firebase Analytics): Retained for up to 14 months per Google's standard retention policy, then automatically deleted
  • No Personal Data Retention: Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to retain or delete on our end

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Tax Master integrates with the following third-party services, all configured for anonymized data collection only:

5.1 Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

  • Purpose: Automatic crash detection and app stability monitoring
  • Data Shared: Anonymized crash logs, device model, OS version, app version — no personal or tax data
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Firebase Analytics (Google)

  • Purpose: Anonymized app usage insights to understand which features are most valuable
  • Data Shared: Anonymized feature usage, session duration, screen views — no personal or tax data
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5.3 Google Play Services

  • Purpose: Core app distribution and updates via Google Play Store
  • Data Shared: Only technical data necessary for app distribution

No Data Sharing with Third Parties: We do not share your tax data or any personal information with financial advisors, accounting firms, insurance companies, or any other third parties. Your tax calculations remain completely private on your device.

6. DATA SECURITY

6.1 Encryption & Storage

  • Local Storage: Tax data stored on your device is protected by Android's built-in encryption if your device has a PIN/pattern/fingerprint lock
  • Data in Transit: Since your tax data never leaves your device, there is no transmission risk. App updates use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • No Cloud Storage: Tax data is not uploaded to our servers or any cloud service

6.2 Security Practices

  • No backend servers storing user data (eliminates server breach risk)
  • Regular software updates with security patches
  • Minimal data collection by design (privacy-first architecture)

6.3 What You Can Do

  • Use a strong device lock (PIN, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keep your Android operating system updated
  • Do not share your device with others while using Tax Master

Limitation of Liability: While we use industry-standard security measures, no system is 100% secure. We are not liable for unauthorized access resulting from device theft, malware, or user negligence.

7. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS (GDPR & BELGIAN LAW)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian Privacy Law, you have the following rights:

7.1 Right of Access

Since we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is no personal data to access. Your tax data is stored only on your device and accessible directly through the App.

7.2 Right to Rectification

For tax data stored locally on your device, you can edit or delete it directly within the App at any time.

7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

Your data is already under your complete control:

  • Uninstall the app — this automatically deletes all locally stored data from your device
  • Since we don't store your personal or tax data on our servers, there is nothing to delete on our end

7.4 Right to Object to Processing

You can limit anonymized analytics and crash reporting by adjusting your preferences through your Android device settings under Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy.
